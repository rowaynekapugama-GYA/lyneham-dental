Crowns and bridges

When a tooth needs more support than a filling can offer, or a gap has been left by a missing tooth, crowns and bridges are two well-established ways to restore your smile to full strength. Both are designed to look natural and to bring back comfort and function, so that you can eat and speak with confidence again.

Crowns & Bridges

Crowns

A crown is a tooth-shaped cap that covers a damaged or weakened tooth entirely, restoring its strength and refining its appearance. Crowns are often recommended for teeth that are heavily filled, cracked, or have had root canal treatment. We are frequently able to make yours in a single visit using our CEREC system, or work with skilled local technicians where that is the better approach. You can read more on our Single-Visit Crowns page.

Bridges

A bridge can replace a missing tooth by securing a natural-looking replacement to the teeth on either side of the gap. By filling the space, a bridge helps restore your bite, supports the surrounding teeth, and returns the appearance of your smile. There are different types of bridge, and the right one depends on your individual circumstances.

Which option is right for you?

Whether a crown, a bridge or another approach is most suitable depends on the condition of the tooth or teeth involved, the health of those around them, and your own preferences. Your dentist will examine your teeth, take any imaging needed, and set out your options clearly, so that you can make an informed decision with confidence. We will only ever recommend what we believe genuinely suits you.

Caring for crowns and bridges

Crowns and bridges are cared for much like natural teeth, though a bridge requires a little extra attention to clean beneath and around it: we will show you how. Regular brushing, daily flossing and routine check-ups all help your restoration last, and we will keep an eye on it at each visit.

If you are considering treatment, the best starting point is a consultation, during which we can examine your teeth, answer your questions, and explain what each option would involve for you. We are also glad to discuss the likely cost and timeframe, so that you can plan with confidence. Whatever you decide, our aim is to help you reach a choice you feel genuinely comfortable with.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between a crown and a bridge?

A crown covers and restores a single damaged tooth, while a bridge replaces a missing tooth by anchoring a replacement to the teeth on either side. Your dentist will advise which suits your situation.

How long do crowns and bridges last?

With good care, both can last many years. Their lifespan depends on factors such as your oral health and habits, and we will monitor them at your regular check-ups.

Is getting a crown or bridge uncomfortable?

We make sure the area is comfortably numb during treatment. Some mild sensitivity afterwards is normal and usually settles quickly.

How do I clean under a bridge?

A bridge needs a little extra care to keep the area beneath it clean. We will show you simple techniques and tools to make this easy as part of your daily routine.

Will my crown or bridge look natural?

Yes, both are designed to match the shade and shape of your surrounding teeth, so the result blends naturally with your smile.

Are there other ways to replace a missing tooth?

There are several ways to restore a gap, and a bridge is one of them. Your dentist will discuss the options suited to your situation and help you choose.
