Single-visit ceramic crowns

A crown is a tailored cap that fits over a damaged or weakened tooth, restoring its strength and appearance and protecting what remains. Crowns are one of the most dependable ways to give a compromised tooth a new lease of life, and at Lyneham Dental Care, we are often able to complete the entire process in a single visit, sparing you the inconvenience of moulds, temporary crowns and a second appointment.

CEREC One-Visit Crowns

When a crown is recommended

Your dentist may suggest a crown when a tooth is significantly weakened, heavily filled, cracked, or has undergone root canal treatment and needs protecting. A crown can also restore a tooth that has worn down or broken. In each case, the aim is the same: to protect and preserve your natural tooth, keeping it strong and comfortable for everyday use.

The benefit of single-visit dentistry

Using the CEREC system, we take a precise digital scan of your tooth, with no need for conventional putty moulds, design your ceramic crown on screen, and craft it here at the practice while you wait. The finished crown is placed the same day, which removes the need for a temporary crown or a return visit. Where CEREC is not the most suitable choice for your particular tooth, we work with skilled local technicians instead, and will always recommend the option that serves you best.

A natural-looking, lasting result

Modern ceramic crowns are designed to look and feel like a natural tooth, matched in shade and shape to blend with your smile. They are strong and hard-wearing, and with good care can last many years. We will check your crown at your regular visits to make sure it continues to serve you well.

Caring for your crown

A crowned tooth is cared for much like any other: regular brushing, daily flossing and routine check-ups will help it last. While crowns are durable, it is wise to avoid biting very hard objects. If you ever notice any discomfort or a change in how your crown feels, please let us know.

Because the entire process is completed in one appointment, a single-visit crown can be a particularly convenient option for busy patients, or for anyone who would simply prefer to avoid the wait and the return trip that a traditional crown involves.

CEREC One-Visit Crowns

Frequently asked questions

What is the advantage of a single-visit crown?

A single-visit crown is designed, made and placed in one appointment, so there is no need for putty moulds, a temporary crown or a second visit. It is a convenient, comfortable option for many patients.

Does getting a crown hurt?

We make sure the tooth is comfortably numb while it is prepared, so the procedure should not hurt. Some mild sensitivity afterwards is normal and usually settles.

How long do ceramic crowns last?

With good care, crowns can last many years. Their lifespan depends on factors such as your oral health and habits, and we will monitor your crown at your regular check-ups.

Will my crown look natural?

Yes, ceramic crowns are matched in shade and shape to blend with your surrounding teeth, so the result looks and feels natural.

Is a single-visit crown suitable for every tooth?

It suits many situations, but not all. Where CEREC is not the best choice for your particular tooth, we work with skilled local technicians instead, and will recommend the most appropriate option for you.

How do I look after a crowned tooth?

Care for it as you would any tooth: regular brushing, daily flossing and routine visits. Avoid biting very hard objects, and let us know if your crown ever feels uncomfortable.
