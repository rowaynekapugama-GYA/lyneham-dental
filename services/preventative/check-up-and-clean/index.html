Preventative

Examinations and professional cleaning

A regular dental examination is the cornerstone of good oral health. It is the appointment that quietly does the most for your smile, allowing us to keep a careful watch on your teeth and gums, to address minor issues before they progress, and to leave you feeling fresh and well cared for. For many people it is also a welcome reset: a chance to ask the small questions that have been on their mind, and to feel confident that everything is in good order.

Check-up & Clean

What a thorough examination involves

Your dentist will carefully examine your teeth, gums and the soft tissues of your mouth, looking for any early signs of decay, gum concerns or other changes that are easier to manage when caught early. This is also an opportunity to talk through anything you have noticed yourself, whether that is sensitivity, a rough edge, or simply a question about your routine. We take the time to explain what we see in plain terms, so you always leave understanding the state of your oral health and what, if anything, is worth keeping an eye on.

Professional cleaning, gently done

Even with diligent brushing and flossing, plaque gradually hardens into tartar in places a toothbrush cannot reach. During your clean, we use professional scaling to remove this build-up, followed by a polish to leave your teeth feeling smooth and refreshed. Keeping tartar in check helps maintain healthy gums and fresher breath, and supports the long-term health of the bone and tissues that hold your teeth in place. Our approach is unhurried and gentle throughout.

Radiographs, when they are needed

From time to time, your dentist may recommend a digital radiograph (x-ray) to see areas not visible to the eye, such as between the teeth or beneath the gumline. Our imaging is digital, which keeps radiation exposure low, and we will only ever take radiographs when they genuinely add to your care, and always with your consent.

How often should you visit?

There is no single rule that suits everyone. How frequently you should attend depends on your individual needs and history, and your dentist will recommend a rhythm tailored to you rather than a fixed schedule. Paired with good habits at home, regular brushing, daily flossing and a sensible diet, consistent visits help keep your teeth and gums healthy for years to come.

Check-up & Clean

Frequently asked questions

How long does a check-up and clean take?

Most appointments take around 30 to 45 minutes, depending on what is involved. If it has been some time since your last visit, or there is more cleaning required, we may allow a little longer so we never feel rushed.

Does a scale and clean hurt?

For most people it is comfortable, though you may feel some sensitivity if your gums are a little inflamed or it has been a while between cleans. Let us know if anything feels tender and we will adjust our approach: keeping you comfortable is always our priority.

How often should I have a dental check-up?

It varies from person to person. Many people attend every six to twelve months, but your dentist will suggest an interval based on your individual needs after examining your teeth and gums.

Are dental x-rays safe?

Our x-rays are digital, which keeps radiation exposure very low. We take them only when they will genuinely help your diagnosis or treatment, and always with your consent. If you are pregnant or have any concerns, please let us know.

Can I claim a check-up and clean through my health fund?

If you hold private health cover with dental, you can claim on the spot through HICAPS, so you settle only the gap. What you can claim back depends on your individual level of cover.

It has been years since I last saw a dentist: will I be judged?

Not at all. We see people in this situation often, and there is no lecture waiting for you. We will simply take a gentle look, talk you through where things stand, and help you move forward at a pace you are comfortable with.
