Preventative

Caring for younger smiles

A child's early experiences at the dentist shape their attitude to oral health for the rest of their life. That is why we take such care to make those first visits calm, friendly and positive, helping children feel at ease, and helping good habits take root early. A child who learns that the dentist is nothing to fear is far more likely to look after their smile as they grow.

Children's Dentistry

Starting at the right age

We generally recommend beginning regular visits around toddler age, once the first teeth have come through. Many families find it helps to bring their child along to a parent's appointment first, so the sights and sounds of the practice become familiar and unintimidating before any treatment is needed. There is no pressure and no rush: early visits are often as much about saying hello as anything else.

Fissure sealants for growing teeth

The biting surfaces of the back teeth contain natural grooves, and on some children these are deep and difficult to clean thoroughly. A fissure sealant is a thin protective coating applied over them to help guard against decay. The treatment is quick and comfortable, with no drilling involved, and applied early it can spare a child the need for fillings later on.

Mouthguards for sport

For children involved in sport, a custom-fitted mouthguard offers important protection for developing teeth, gums and jaws, and is compulsory in many sporting codes. A guard made to fit your child's mouth precisely is more comfortable and more secure than an off-the-shelf version, which means it is far more likely to be worn. We can make one in a choice of colours to suit your child.

The Child Dental Benefits Schedule

The Child Dental Benefits Schedule (CDBS) is a government scheme providing eligible children with dental benefits, and we currently bulk bill for children who qualify. Children aged 2 to 17 may be eligible if your family receives Family Tax Benefit Part A or certain government payments. We recommend confirming your child's eligibility with Medicare, and please mention it when booking so we can make the necessary arrangements.

Children's Dentistry

Frequently asked questions

When should my child first see a dentist?

Around toddler age is a good time to begin, once the first teeth appear. Early, relaxed visits help your child grow comfortable with the dentist before any treatment is ever needed.

How can I prepare my child for their first visit?

Keep it positive and low-key, and avoid words that might worry them. Bringing them along to your own appointment first can help, as can letting them know the dentist is simply going to count and check their teeth.

Are sealants and fillings painful for children?

Sealants involve no drilling and are very comfortable. If a filling is ever needed, we use gentle techniques and take great care to keep your child relaxed and at ease throughout.

How do I know if my child is eligible for the CDBS?

Children aged 2 to 17 may be eligible if your family receives Family Tax Benefit Part A or certain government payments. The simplest way to confirm is to check with Medicare, and we are happy to help where we can.

How can I help prevent cavities in my child's teeth?

Regular brushing with the right amount of fluoride toothpaste, limiting sugary snacks and drinks, and routine dental visits all make a real difference. We are glad to offer tailored advice for your child at their appointment.

My child is anxious about the dentist: can you help?

Absolutely. We see anxious children often and adjust our pace and approach to suit each child. Our aim is always to build trust gently, so that visits become easier over time.
