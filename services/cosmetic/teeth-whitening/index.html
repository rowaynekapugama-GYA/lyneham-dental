Cosmetic

Teeth whitening

Teeth whitening is a refined and straightforward way to lighten the colour of your teeth and freshen your smile, whether in preparation for a special occasion, or simply to address the gradual discolouration that comes with everyday life. It is one of the gentler cosmetic treatments, and for many people a noticeable lift to their confidence comes from a relatively small change.

Teeth Whitening

Why teeth become discoloured

Teeth darken and stain for all sorts of reasons. Everyday culprits include tea, coffee, red wine and certain foods, while age, some medications and natural variation all play a part too. Understanding the cause of your discolouration matters, because it helps us advise whether whitening is likely to achieve the result you have in mind.

Our tailored, take-home approach

We favour a gentle, professional take-home method. After assessing your teeth, we create custom-made trays designed to fit them precisely, along with a whitening gel for you to use at home according to our guidance. The custom fit means the gel is held evenly against your teeth, which supports a more consistent, comfortable result, and lets you whiten at your own pace in your own time.

Is whitening right for you?

Whitening is not suitable for everyone, and it is important to be realistic about what it can achieve. It will not change the colour of existing fillings, crowns or veneers, and certain types of discolouration respond better than others. This is precisely why your dentist will examine your teeth first, so we can advise whether whitening is appropriate for you and what result is realistic, rather than promising an outcome we cannot be sure of. Some patients experience temporary sensitivity, which we will discuss with you beforehand.

Keeping your smile bright

Whitening is not permanent, and your results will gradually fade as everyday foods and drinks take their toll. Good habits help, and your custom trays can often be used for occasional top-ups down the track. We are happy to advise on maintaining your result at your regular visits.

Throughout the process, we are here to answer your questions and make sure you feel confident using your kit at home. If at any point you are unsure, or notice anything unexpected, simply get in touch and we will be glad to help.

Teeth Whitening

Frequently asked questions

Is professional teeth whitening safe?

When carried out under a dentist's guidance, whitening is a well-established treatment. We assess your teeth first to make sure it is suitable for you, and explain how to use your kit safely and effectively.

Will whitening work on my teeth?

It depends on the type and cause of discolouration, which is why we examine your teeth first. Whitening will not change the colour of fillings, crowns or veneers, and some stains respond better than others: we will give you an honest view of what is realistic.

Does teeth whitening cause sensitivity?

Some people experience temporary sensitivity during or after whitening, which usually settles. We will talk you through how to manage this before you begin.

How long do whitening results last?

Results are not permanent and gradually fade as everyday foods and drinks stain the teeth again. With good habits and occasional top-ups using your custom trays, you can help maintain your result.

Why not just use an over-the-counter whitening kit?

Custom trays made by your dentist fit precisely and hold the gel evenly against your teeth, which supports a safer, more even result. A dental assessment first also ensures whitening is suitable for you.

Can I whiten my teeth if I have fillings or crowns?

Whitening does not change the colour of existing fillings, crowns or veneers, so these may stand out afterwards. Your dentist will take this into account and discuss your options with you.
