General

Tooth-coloured fillings

A filling is often the simplest and most effective way to restore a tooth affected by decay or minor damage. It is one of the most common treatments in dentistry, and, done well, one of the most quietly satisfying, returning a tooth to comfort and function with very little fuss. Because we use tooth-coloured materials, the repair blends naturally with your existing teeth, so the result is discreet and indistinguishable from the rest of your smile.

Tooth-Coloured Fillings

When a filling is needed

Decay, a small chip or a crack can all create a space where a tooth begins to break down. Left unattended, these small problems tend to grow, which is why early treatment is so worthwhile: a minor repair today is almost always simpler, quicker and gentler than a larger one later. Signs worth having checked include sensitivity to hot or cold, a rough edge your tongue keeps finding, mild discomfort when chewing, or a visible mark or hole in a tooth.

What to expect during treatment

Your dentist will first make sure the area is comfortably numb, then gently remove any decay and clean the tooth before placing and shaping the filling material. Once set, the filling is adjusted so that your bite feels natural and even. Most fillings are completed in a single, straightforward appointment, and we take care to keep you relaxed and comfortable throughout.

Caring for your tooth afterwards

Your tooth may feel a little sensitive for a short while after a filling, which usually settles on its own. Looking after a filled tooth is much like looking after any other: regular brushing, daily flossing and routine check-ups will help it last. We will keep an eye on your fillings at each visit and let you know if any ever need attention.

Our approach

Our guiding principle is always to preserve as much of your natural tooth as possible, and to recommend only what is genuinely required. We will examine the tooth, explain honestly what we find, and talk you through your options so that you can decide with confidence.

If you feel anxious about dental treatment, please do let us know. We look after many patients who find appointments daunting, and we will happily take things at a pace that suits you, explaining each step, and pausing whenever you need a moment.

Frequently asked questions

Does getting a filling hurt?

We make sure the area is comfortably numb before we begin, so the procedure itself should not hurt. Some mild sensitivity afterwards is normal and usually settles within a few days.

How do I know if I need a filling?

Common signs include sensitivity, a rough edge, discomfort when chewing, or a visible mark on a tooth, but decay can also be present without symptoms. A regular check-up is the most reliable way to catch it early.

Are tooth-coloured fillings as strong as silver ones?

Modern tooth-coloured materials are strong, durable and bond well to the tooth. Your dentist will recommend the most suitable material for the size and position of your filling.

How long will a filling last?

With good care, fillings can last many years, though no filling lasts forever. Regular check-ups allow us to monitor them and replace any that wear over time.

Why does my tooth still feel sensitive after a filling?

Some sensitivity after a filling is common and usually settles within a week or two. If it persists or worsens, please get in touch so we can check the tooth.

Can a filling fix a chipped tooth?

Often, yes, a tooth-coloured filling can repair a small chip discreetly. For larger damage, your dentist may suggest an alternative such as a crown, and will talk you through the options.
