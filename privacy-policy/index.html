Privacy policy

How Lyneham Dental Care collects, uses and protects your personal and health information.

1. About this policy

Lyneham Dental Care is a private dental practice at 45 Hall Street, Lyneham ACT 2602. This policy explains how we collect, use, store and share your personal information, including your health information, and how you can see or correct it.

We handle personal information in line with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth), including the Australian Privacy Principles, and the Health Records (Privacy and Access) Act 1997 (ACT). The policy applies to patients, parents and guardians of patients, people who contact us, and visitors to our website.

You can ask us for a free copy of this policy in another form, such as a printed copy, at reception or by calling 02 6249 1785.

2. What we collect and how

We collect the information we need to provide safe, appropriate dental care and to run the practice. This may include:

  • Contact and identity details: your name, date of birth, address, phone number, email address and emergency contact
  • Health information: your medical and dental history, medications, allergies, examination notes, radiographs (x-rays), photographs, scans, treatment plans and records of the care we provide
  • Payment and claim details: your health fund, Medicare number (for the Child Dental Benefits Schedule), Department of Veterans’ Affairs (DVA) details and payment records
  • For children: the details of a parent or guardian

We usually collect this information from you directly: when you book, fill in our patient forms, attend an appointment, call or email us, send an enquiry through our website, or book online.

Sometimes we receive information from others, such as a referring dentist or doctor, a specialist, a health fund, Medicare or DVA, or a parent, guardian or carer. We only do this where it is needed for your care or you would reasonably expect it.

You can ask to deal with us anonymously or under a different name, for example for a general enquiry. For treatment and claims, we need your real details.

3. How we use and share your information

We use your information to:

  • assess, diagnose and treat you, and plan your care
  • send appointment confirmations, reminders and recall notices by phone, SMS or email
  • process payments and claims with your health fund through HICAPS, and with Medicare (Child Dental Benefits Schedule) or DVA where they apply
  • meet our legal, professional and insurance obligations, and improve the quality and safety of our care

We only share your information where it is needed for your care, where you have agreed, or where the law requires or allows it. This may include:

  • specialists, doctors and other health practitioners involved in your care, such as when we refer you
  • dental laboratories that make crowns, bridges, mouthguards and other appliances
  • your health fund, Medicare and DVA, to process claims
  • the service providers who help us run the practice, such as our practice management and online booking system (Zavy), IT support and our website and email providers, who may only use the information to provide their service to us
  • our insurers and professional advisers, and government bodies where the law requires it

We do not sell your personal information.

Marketing. We will only send you newsletters or promotional messages if you have agreed to receive them, and you can opt out at any time by replying STOP, using the unsubscribe link or telling us. Appointment reminders and recall notices are part of your care, not marketing.

4. Storage, security and how long we keep records

Our patient records are digital and are stored in an Australian-based cloud database. We protect them with password-protected accounts, access limited to team members who need it, secure systems and staff confidentiality obligations. Any paper documents are kept securely and destroyed securely when no longer needed.

Information sent overseas. Patient records are stored in Australia. If you send an enquiry through our website, your message passes through our website host and email delivery service before it reaches us, and these providers may process it outside Australia, including in the United States. Please don’t include detailed health information in a website enquiry; we’ll discuss that with you directly.

How long we keep records. As required by ACT law, we keep health records for at least seven years after the last entry for adults. For a patient who was under 18 at the time of treatment, we keep them until they turn 25. After that, records are destroyed securely.

Data breaches. If a data breach is likely to cause you serious harm, we will notify you and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner, as required by the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme.

5. Our website

  • Enquiry form: when you send an enquiry, we receive your name, phone number, email address, preferred contact method, message and the page you sent it from. We use these only to reply to you.
  • Online booking: our Book Online page uses Zavy, our practice management system. The details you enter there go straight into our booking system to make your appointment, and Zavy handles them on our behalf.
  • Map and fonts: our map and the fonts on our website are provided by Google. When a page loads them, your browser connects to Google, which may record your IP address and set cookies under its own privacy policy.
  • Cookies and analytics: we do not currently use analytics or advertising cookies on our website. If we add tools such as Google Analytics, we will update this policy first.

Our website links to other websites, such as health funds and government services. We are not responsible for their privacy practices.

6. Access, correction and complaints

Seeing your records. You can ask to see your records or get a copy, in writing or at reception. We will respond within 14 days, as ACT law requires. We may need to confirm your identity first, and a fee may apply for copies or a summary, within the limits set under ACT law; we will tell you before any fee is charged. In limited cases the law allows us to refuse access, and we will explain why in writing.

Correcting your information. If any of your details are wrong or out of date, please tell us and we will correct them. If we disagree that a health record needs changing, you can ask us to attach your statement to it.

Questions and complaints. Please contact us first so we can try to resolve your concern. We aim to respond within 30 days.

  • Lyneham Dental Care, 45 Hall Street, Lyneham ACT 2602
  • Phone: 02 6249 1785
  • Email: admin@lynehamdentalcare.com.au

If you are not satisfied with our response, you can contact:

  • ACT Human Rights Commission (health services and health records in the ACT): 02 6205 2222, hrc.act.gov.au
  • Office of the Australian Information Commissioner: 1300 363 992, oaic.gov.au

Changes to this policy. We may update this policy from time to time. The current version is always on our website.
