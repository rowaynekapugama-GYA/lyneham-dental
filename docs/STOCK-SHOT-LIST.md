# Lyneham Dental Care: photos to buy or supply

Written 19 November 2026 for v1.6.0, updated 23 November 2026 for v1.7.0 and v1.7.1. All five slots are now filled. The docs folder is not deployed.

Rules for every photo:
- Licensed stock that GYA has bought, or a photo the practice took. Nothing downloaded from the web, and no AI-generated images.
- Landscape, at least 2000 px wide, realistic and clinically accurate.
- No branding or logos, and no before and after claims.

All five slots are filled (see Status). To swap a photo in, save it as WebP at 1600 x 900 under the same file name, plus a 240 x 240 square crop as the `-thumb` file where one is listed. Or send the photo to GYA and we'll prepare both files.
